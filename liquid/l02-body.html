HOME MUSIC LIQUID · the liquid room of the house: same 24-hour programme, morning 06-13, afternoon 13-20, night 20-06, same crates, no stage, no ceremony. The background is the sound itself: a liquid field breathing with the levels and the tempo of what is playing. Tracks stream via YouTube, every tune linked to YouTube and Spotify. Liner notes compiled from Wikipedia, Discogs, AllMusic and the music press. The main room, with the selector and the rite of the 45, lives at the station's front door. Shortcuts: [→] next · [m] mute.
