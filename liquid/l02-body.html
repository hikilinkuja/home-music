HOME MUSIC LIQUID · the liquid room of the house: its own 24-hour programme in seven blocks, from Dawn (06-08) to After Hours (02-06), drawn from the same crates as the main room, with no stage and no ceremony. The background is the sound itself: a liquid field breathing with the levels and the tempo of what is playing. Tracks stream via YouTube, every tune linked to YouTube and Spotify. Liner notes draw mainly on Discogs and Wikipedia. The main room, with the selector and the rite of the 45, lives at the station's front door. Shortcuts: [→] earn a skip · [m] mute · [f] full screen · [v] visuals · [w] art wing.
