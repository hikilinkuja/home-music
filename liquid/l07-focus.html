    Break: a fifth of the block (5 min after 25, 10 after 50). Long break: 30 min after every 3rd block. In the last minute the music fades and you can add 5 minutes. A new length applies from the next block.

    Your experiment

    which sound works for you?

    Compare two sounds for one kind of work. The room alternates them block by block (A B, then B A), and after each block you rate your focus with one tap. The result waits for 4 rated blocks of each.

    Limits: you know which sound is on (no blinding), so expectations count; the rating is your own impression, not a measure of output; and tasks vary from block to block. The result is about you, under these conditions.
