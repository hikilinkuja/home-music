Dub

what you are hearing, and why · every block below is labelled with its source
house writing · the connective tissue

Dub is the song taken apart and reassembled as weather. Around 1973 King Tubby and Lee Perry discovered the mixing desk was an instrument: drop the vocal, drown the drums in echo, let the bass walk around the room like it pays rent. Every remix in existence descends from these board rides.

In their words

quotation · Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0
Dub is a musical style that grew out of reggae in the late 1960s and early 1970s. It is commonly considered a subgenre of reggae, though it has developed to extend beyond that style.
Wikipedia, Dub music, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 · source ↗
King Tubby interchanged the vocals and the instrumental, playing the vocals first, then playing the riddim, then mixing them together.
