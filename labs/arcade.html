Drop the Needle

The needle lands somewhere in the middle of a record from the station's own crates. Six seconds, then it lifts. Name the sound. Three wrong answers and the session ends.

Selector's Shift

Programme one hour of the station. Pick a block, then build a run of six genres from its crate. House rule: neighbouring genres may never jump more than 24 BPM. A valid shift goes on air: the booth plays one tune from each of your genres, in your order.

Dub Desk

The board as instrument, Tubby's way: pull the low end, open the echo, throw a phrase into space. Live performance processing in your browser; nothing is recorded, nothing is redistributed.
